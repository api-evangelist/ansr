Choosing a UK-based Nearshore/Offshore center in India or Poland really boils down to three key priorities – cost efficiency, time zone alignment and scale of talent. Poland offers 1 to 2 hours’ time difference and EU regulatory compliance, while India provides niche workforce and operational arbitrage.
